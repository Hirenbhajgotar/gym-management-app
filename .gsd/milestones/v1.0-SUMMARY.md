# Milestone: v1.0 — Biometric Consent Notification (Email & WhatsApp)

## Completed: 2026-10-11

## Deliverables
- ✅ **Header Action for All Eligible Members**: `RequestBiometricConsentAction` on `ListGymMembers` page to send biometric consent notifications to all active members of the tenant who lack active consent.
- ✅ **Table Bulk Action for Selected Members**: Bulk action on `GymMemberResource` allowing staff to select specific members and trigger biometric consent notifications.
- ✅ **Dual-Gated WhatsApp Channel Availability**: `NotificationChannelResolver::canSendBiometricConsentViaWhatsApp($gym)` dynamically enforces:
  1. Tenant WhatsApp enabled and verified sender number configured.
  2. Approved `biometric_consent` WhatsApp template available.
- ✅ **Dynamic Channel Selection Modal**: Confirmation modal with Email toggle and conditional WhatsApp toggle (rendered ONLY when dual-gate passes), live recipient count, and unified send button.
- ✅ **Two-Tier Queued Fan-Out Pipeline**: Asynchronous background jobs (`FanOutBiometricConsentRequestsJob` chunked at 200 + `SendBiometricConsentNotificationJob` per recipient on `config('queue.notifications_queue')`) preventing HTTP request timeouts on large member rosters.
- ✅ **Consent Idempotency & Eligibility Guard**: `BiometricConsentEligibility` strictly skips already consented (`hasActiveBiometricConsent() === true`), archived, or opted-out members.
- ✅ **Centralized Signed URL Generation**: `BiometricConsentUrlFactory` produces secure, tamper-proof 7-day signed routes (`consent.biometric.show`).
- ✅ **Member Welcome Email Biometric Notice**: Onboarding `WelcomeNotification` incorporates `InteractsWithBiometricConsent` to conditionally embed a biometric attendance notice with 7-day signed consent link when the gym has active biometric hardware (`Gym::hasActiveBiometricDevice()`).
- ✅ **Dynamic Queue Worker Command**: `app:work-queues` (`WorkQueuesCommand`) to poll derived queues (`default`, `notifications`, `broadcast`) based on configuration without hardcoded values.
- ✅ **Full Test Suite & Static Analysis**: 90/90 feature and unit tests passing (476 assertions) on `gym_app_test`, 0 PHPStan errors at Level Max with Strict Rules, and Laravel Pint clean.
- ✅ **Architectural Documentation**: Formalized system architecture in [ADR-0003: Multi-Channel Biometric Consent Distribution and Dual-Gated Notification Architecture](file:///docs/adr/adr-0003-biometric-consent-distribution-and-dual-gate-architecture.md).

## Lessons Learned & Key Architectural Decisions
- **ADR-001: Modal Channel Selection UX**: Combined multi-channel options into a single reactive modal with dynamic conditional visibility for optional channels.
- **ADR-002: Strict Targeting of Unconsented Active Members**: Pre-filtering members with `hasActiveBiometricConsent() === false` prevents compliance spam and friction.
- **ADR-003: WhatsApp Delivery Strategy**: Strict adherence to pre-approved WhatsApp templates outside 24h window with dev/sandbox text fallback ensures zero API rejections while remaining testable locally.
- **ADR-004: Dual-Gated Availability Predicate**: Validating both tenant credentials AND template approval in a dedicated service method prevents runtime job delivery failures.
- **ADR-0003: Asynchronous Two-Tier Fan-Out**: Offloading fan-out to chunked queue jobs completely decouples UI responsiveness from gym member counts.
